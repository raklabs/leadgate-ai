# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **IntelligentStack AI (`@raklabs`)** open-source projects pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy engineering community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing technical opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code-review feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall engineering quality and security of the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Submitting malicious code, supply-chain payloads, CI resource-exhaustion scripts, or automated spam Pull Requests
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers (`@raklabs`) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, including rejecting Pull Requests, locking issues, and blocking abusive accounts.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.
